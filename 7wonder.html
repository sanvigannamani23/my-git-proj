Seven Wonders of the World

Image Wonder Country Description
Chichen Itza Mexico Chichen Itza is an ancient Mayan city in Mexico. It is famous for the El Castillo pyramid and was one of the greatest centers of the Mayan civilization.
Colosseum Italy The Colosseum is a large amphitheater in Rome, Italy. It was used for gladiator fights and public entertainment during the Roman Empire.
Christ the Redeemer Brazil Christ the Redeemer is a giant statue of Jesus Christ located in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It is a symbol of peace and Christianity.
Great Wall of China China The Great Wall of China is one of the longest walls ever built. It was constructed to protect China from invasions.
Machu Picchu Peru Machu Picchu is an ancient Incan city located high in the Andes Mountains of Peru. It is known for its beautiful stone structures.
Petra Jordan Petra is an ancient city in Jordan carved into pink sandstone cliffs. It is famous for its magnificent rock-cut architecture.
Taj Mahal India The Taj Mahal is a white marble monument in Agra, India. It was built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
